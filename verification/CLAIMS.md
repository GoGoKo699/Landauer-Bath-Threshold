## Assumptions that must travel with T1

The memory dimension, temperature, and baseline gap are fixed. The bath is a product Gibbs state of the specified pairs, independent of the memory and any inaccessible reference. The detunings are positive, smaller than the baseline gap, and tend to zero. Arbitrary predetermined global driving is allowed; there is no additional entropy-carrying ancilla, catalyst, measurement record, or refreshed reservoir. Heat is the complete bath's mean energy gain, with the memory degenerate and interactions off at the boundaries.

Nonresonance is required for necessity. The constructive implication holds without it. The exact optimal crossover, efficient circuit implementation, and a sufficiency theorem for arbitrary unpaired baths lie outside the established result. See [model.md](../science/model.md) for the full operational definitions.
